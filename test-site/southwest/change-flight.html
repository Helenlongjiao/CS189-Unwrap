Change flight rules

You may cancel for any reason within 24 hours of booking for a full refund when the flight is at least 7 days away.

Wanna Get Away® fares have no same-day change. Wanna Get Away Plus® and Anytime allow same-day confirmed changes when space is available.

There is no change fee on this demo airline; you only pay any fare difference.
